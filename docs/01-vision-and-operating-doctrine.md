# Vision And Operating Doctrine

## Vision

ai-company should become the operating system a solo SaaS founder trusts to keep the company moving while they are not actively working.

The founder should be able to set intent, constraints, policies, and priorities, then wake up to meaningful progress that is inspectable, reversible where possible, and ready for focused review.

## The Overnight Work Model

The target experience is an overnight work cycle:

1. The founder sets intent and policy.
2. Agents retrieve company context.
3. Agents plan and execute work inside approved boundaries.
4. Risky or uncertain actions are escalated.
5. Quality gates run before work advances.
6. Production actions happen only when policy permits them.
7. The founder receives a clear morning review with decisions, actions, evidence, risks, and next approvals.

This is the heart of the product.

## Operating Doctrine

The product should make agents more capable without making them less accountable.

Agents are not magic background jobs. They are permissioned actors operating through typed capabilities, explicit policies, logged context, reviewable outputs, and auditable state transitions.

## The Three Loops

ai-company should eventually operate through three reinforcing loops.

Context loop: the system continuously improves its understanding of the company, product, architecture, users, operations, decisions, risks, and preferences.

Work loop: the system turns founder intent into scoped work packets, artifacts, staged changes, approvals, and quality checks.

Execution loop: the system performs approved actions against real systems, observes outcomes, records evidence, and updates company context.

## Product Shape

The first product should feel less like a chatbot and more like a company operations console.

The core surfaces should eventually include:

- company context
- work queue
- work packet detail
- agent runs
- approval queue
- policy console
- capability registry
- audit log
- quality results
- integration console
- morning review

These surfaces are conceptual during the seed phase. They are not implementation commitments until the stack and architecture decisions are made.

## Autonomy Philosophy

The goal is not to ask the founder for permission on every minor step.

The goal is to let the founder define standing policy so safe, expected, reversible, low-risk work proceeds without interruption, while uncertain, high-impact, irreversible, or policy-violating work escalates.

## Why This Is Different

ai-company is not trying to be the best prompt box.

It is trying to be the best governed execution environment for AI company work.

The distinction matters because the hard problems are not only generation. The hard problems are scope, memory, trust, execution, rollback, evidence, review, quality, and maintaining production systems over time.

## Success State

The product succeeds when the founder can say:

- I know what the agents did.
- I know why they did it.
- I know what policy allowed it.
- I know what evidence supports it.
- I know what still needs my judgment.
- I trust the system enough to let it keep working overnight.
